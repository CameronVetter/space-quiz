# Project rules

- Keep the quiz self-contained in `index.html`; do not add a server, dependencies, or build tooling.
- Preserve the responsive, narrow-column layout and support both light and dark color schemes.
- Keep controls semantic and keyboard accessible; provide visible focus and clear feedback.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for animations.
- Keep the quiz at 10 questions and preserve progress, scoring, feedback, results, and replay behavior.
- After UI or interaction changes, open `index.html` in the integrated browser and smoke-test the affected flow.
